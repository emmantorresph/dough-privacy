Privacy Policy

Dough · Last updated: 13 October 2026

Dough is a budgeting app by Emmanuel Torres.

The short version: Dough has no account and no server. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. We never see it.

What Dough stores

Your expenses, income, budgets, bills, savings goals and your own categories, plus the items and totals from receipts you scan.

Where it's kept

On your iPhone, and synced to your private iCloud through Apple's CloudKit. Only you can access it — we can't.

Scanning receipts

Text is read on your iPhone by Apple's on-device recognition. The photo is never uploaded or saved; only the items and totals are kept.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to scan. If you pick a photo instead, Dough receives only that one image, not the rest of your library.

What we don't do

No ads. No analytics. No tracking. No third-party software. We don't sell or share your data, because we never receive it.

iCloud

Sync is provided by Apple and covered by Apple's privacy policy. You can turn it off for Dough in iOS Settings.

Deleting your data

Delete all my data in Dough's Settings removes everything from your iPhone and your iCloud. This can't be undone. Deleting the app removes only the copy on your iPhone; your iCloud copy stays until you use Delete all my data.

Children

Dough isn't directed at children under 13.
